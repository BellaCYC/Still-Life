Get it on your device

Windows

Laptops and desktops, Windows 10 and 11

  1. Download StillLife-Setup.exe.
  2. Double-click it. If you see "Windows protected your PC", see below.
  3. StillLife opens; find it later in the Start menu.
Download
THIS DEVICE

Mac

MacBook and iMac, Apple silicon and Intel

  1. Download StillLife-Mac.dmg, open it, drag StillLife into Applications.
  2. Open StillLife from Applications.
  3. If the Mac says it can't check the developer: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
Download
THIS DEVICE

iPhone and iPad

Opens full screen with its own icon, works offline

  1. Open the StillLife app page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
Open StillLife
THIS DEVICE

Android

Samsung Galaxy phones and tablets, and other Android devices

  1. Open the StillLife app page in Chrome or Samsung Internet.
  2. Tap ⋮ (or ≡ in Samsung Internet).
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
Open StillLife

Your notes stay on the device you write them on. To move them to another device, use Back up all notes in StillLife, then Import the file on the other one.
